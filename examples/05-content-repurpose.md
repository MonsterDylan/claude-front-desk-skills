## Post 3: What is ApoB?

**Hook:** What is ApoB?

**Script (about 91 words):**
ApoB is a blood test that counts the particles in your blood that can carry cholesterol into your artery walls. A standard cholesterol panel measures how much cholesterol those particles hold. ApoB counts the particles themselves. Two people can have the same LDL and a different ApoB, which is why some doctors like to look at both. It's one piece of information, not a diagnosis, and it only means something alongside the rest of your history. If you're curious whether it belongs in your labs, ask us at your next visit.

**Caption:**
What is ApoB? It's a blood test that counts cholesterol-carrying particles, not just the cholesterol.
It's one piece of the picture, not a diagnosis.
Curious if it belongs in your labs? Ask at your next visit.
Dr. Sample and the Harbor team

**Format:** Carousel, about four slides: the question, standard cholesterol panel versus ApoB, "one piece of the picture", and ask at your next visit. A simple visual of particles versus the cholesterol they carry explains this faster than talking.

**Check before posting:**
- Dr. Sample should confirm the explanation and that ApoB is part of Harbor's lab panels.
- The script makes no claim that ApoB prevents or catches heart disease early. Keep it that way, because that would read as a promise of results.
- Reviewer per your profile: Dr. Sample, because this is a labs post.
