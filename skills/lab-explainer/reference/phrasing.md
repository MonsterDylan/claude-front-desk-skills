# Phrasing for lab notes

## What a test measures (one sentence each)
- Vitamin D: "Vitamin D helps your bones, muscles and immune system."
- ApoB: "ApoB counts the particles that carry cholesterol through your blood. It's one of the markers doctors use to look at heart health."
- LDL cholesterol: "LDL is the type of cholesterol that can build up in blood vessels."
- HbA1c: "HbA1c shows your average blood sugar over the last two to three months."
- Fasting glucose: "Fasting glucose is your blood sugar level after not eating overnight."
- TSH: "TSH is a hormone that tells your thyroid how hard to work."
- Ferritin: "Ferritin shows how much iron your body has stored."
- hs-CRP: "hs-CRP is a marker of inflammation in the body."
- Testosterone (total): "Testosterone is a hormone that affects energy, muscle, mood and more."

## Works
- "Your vitamin D is below the lab's range."
- "This one is higher than the range the lab uses."
- "Yours is 22 ng/mL, and the lab's range is 30 to 100."
- "Dr. Sample will go over these with you at your visit." (once, after all the out-of-range results)
- "Everything else on your panel came back within the lab's ranges."

## Avoid
- "Nothing to worry about" / "not urgent" / "totally fine" (only the doctor decides that)
- "You should start taking..." / "Try cutting out..." (treatment advice)
- "This means you have..." (diagnosis)
- "Slightly", "just a bit", "barely" (minimizing)
- "Alarming", "dangerous", "concerning" (alarming)
